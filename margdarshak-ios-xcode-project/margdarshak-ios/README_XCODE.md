====================================================================
MARGDARSHAK (मार्गदर्शक) — iOS (iPHONE & iPAD) INSTALLATION GUIDE
====================================================================

Because Apple iOS restricts direct installation of unsigned .ipa files
from web browsers, we have included 3 complete ways to install and run
Margdarshak on your iPhone or iPad:

--------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 1: INSTANT iOS HOME SCREEN APP (NO COMPUTER / NO APPLE ID NEEDED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open `Margdarshak-iOS-App.html` in Safari on your iPhone/iPad (or
   open the Google Drive link in Safari).
2. Tap the Safari Share icon [↑] at the bottom of the screen.
3. Tap "Add to Home Screen" -> "Add".
4. Margdarshak is now installed on your iPhone Home Screen with its
   custom Deep Teal & Saffron icon and runs full-screen offline!

--------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 2: APPLE iOS WEBCLIP PROFILE (.mobileconfig)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open `Margdarshak-iOS-WebClip.mobileconfig` in Safari or Files on
   your iPhone.
2. Open the iOS Settings app -> tap "Profile Downloaded" at the top.
3. Tap "Install" in the top-right corner to add the Margdarshak WebClip
   to your iOS Home Screen.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 3: NATIVE iOS APP VIA XCODE OR SIDELOADLY / ALTSTORE (.ipa)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- To install via Xcode on a Mac:
  1. Extract `margdarshak-ios-xcode-project.zip`.
  2. Open `Margdarshak.xcodeproj` in Xcode.
  3. Select your personal Apple ID under Signing & Capabilities (free).
  4. Connect your iPhone and press Cmd + R (Run) to install the native
     WKWebView iOS app directly onto your iPhone!
